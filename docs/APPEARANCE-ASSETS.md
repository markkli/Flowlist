# Appearance assets

The Coast and Hills assets were generated with the built-in image-generation tool on September 20, 2026, then encoded as WebP for delivery. No CLI fallback or external image hosting was used. Grove is the existing app artwork. Each landscape is shared by the Pomodoro and Plan covers with different crops and legibility overlays; both artwork switches are independent. Solid presets use theme-specific flat colors.

Final assets:
- `frontend/public/images/focus-coast.webp`
- `frontend/public/images/focus-hills.webp`
- Existing `frontend/public/images/focus-grove.webp`
- Code-native vector brand: `frontend/public/brand/flowlist-mark.svg` and `frontend/public/favicon.svg`

Coast prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: atmospheric landscape background for Flowlist, an elegant personal focus app. Create a wide 2:1 editorial photograph of a tranquil silver-blue coastal sea under a warm overcast sky, distant mist-softened headland on the far right, tiny gentle ripples, soft stone and slate palette, spacious composition and broad quiet negative space especially on the left for UI text and clock. Fine subtle film grain, natural low contrast, restrained premium photography. No buildings, people, boats, typography, logo, border, or watermark. This is a full-bleed image asset, not an app mockup.

Hills prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: atmospheric landscape background for Flowlist, an elegant personal focus app. Create a wide 2:1 editorial photograph of rolling dry-grass hills at dawn with a softly sunlit ridge on the right and pale hazy mountain layers behind it, warm limestone, muted taupe, dusty olive, parchment palette, quiet broad tonal areas on the left for UI text and a clock. Refined natural landscape photography with subtle film grain and low contrast. No people, roads, buildings, typography, logo, border, or watermark. Full-bleed image asset only, not a UI mockup.

The icon uses an upright serif F, an ink tile, ivory lettering, and a restrained brass frame. It is a vector asset, not generated raster artwork.
