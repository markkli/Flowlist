About your data in this beta

Flowlist stores your projects, tasks, focus history, and reflections in your private account. Supabase handles sign-in, including Google when enabled. Google sign-in requests only your basic profile and email address. Unsaved timers stay on this browser; appearance settings stay on this device.

AI enrichment is disabled in the hosted beta. Export your saved data from History. You can delete your account and app data from the account menu. Backups may retain deleted records according to the hosting provider’s retention period.

Keep this tab open for interval reminders. Browser sleep can delay them. This is beta software; keep an export of work you need.
